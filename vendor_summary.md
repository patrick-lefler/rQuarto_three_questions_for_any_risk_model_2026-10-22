# RiskMetric Systems — VendorScore Methodology Summary

*Fictional vendor disclosure used in the NorthPoint Bank case study.*

**Product.** VendorScore produces a monthly incident-probability score for
each third-party vendor in a client's portfolio.

**Method.** VendorScore uses ensemble machine learning on proprietary threat
intelligence. Model structure, training data and feature definitions are
confidential.

**Performance.** VendorScore achieves an AUC of 0.81 on vendor development
data.

**Calibration.** Scores are well-calibrated: a score of 10% corresponds to an
observed incident rate of about 10%.

**Delivery.** Clients receive scores only, refreshed monthly.
